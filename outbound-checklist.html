Sevenum NL
  1. Delivery InfoType your name, the delivery #, and select the destination (Europe ≤180cm / Export ≤160cm).
  2. Part NumberScan or type the PN from the first — or any — Pick Label. It's checked against every pallet and box.
  3. Pallet SetupPick the pallet type (KP/EP/BP/GP/NP/AP), enter weight (kg) and height (cm).
  4. Pick LabelsScan the piece quantities on the pallet's Pick Labels (Pick 1, Pick 2…) — their sum is the target.
  5. Box VerificationScan the PN from the first — or any — box label, then scan each box's quantity in sequence.
  6. More PalletsAdd Pallet 2, 3… and repeat Pallet Setup → Pick Labels → Box Verification for each.
  7. PrintOnce every pallet is complete and matching, press Print. Missing data or mismatched quantities block the report.
