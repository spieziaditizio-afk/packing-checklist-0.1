Scan the whole row

Box Verification · Scan mode

Load every pallet in the delivery first — type, weight, height, and each pick label's target. Then start scanning at pallet 1 and don't stop: the app reads every scan in real time and jumps to the next pallet on its own the moment a target is reached. No walking back to the computer between pallets.

PHASE 1 · LOAD THE WHOLE ROW FIRST

Before a single box gets scanned, every pallet in the delivery gets its data entered once — this is what the app checks each scan against later.

PALLET 1 EP · 420 kg · 165 cm target 250 pcs
PALLET 2 EP · 365 kg · 150 cm target 180 pcs

PHASE 2 · THEN SCAN STRAIGHT THROUGH

Scan the part number off the first box, then the quantity of every box after it. The app totals the pallet as you go — you never pick which pallet is next.

You scan

  • The part number off the first box of each pallet
  • The quantity of every box after that, one after another

The app does the rest

  • Totals every scan against that pallet's target, live
  • Switches tabs and focuses the next pallet's scan field on its own
IF A SCAN GOES OVER TARGET

The auto-jump only fires on an exact match. If a scan pushes a pallet's total past its target instead, the row doesn't move on — it stops right there and shows this, in place of the jump:

Pallet 3 · Box Verification · Scan mode
⚠ OVER TARGET Pallet 3 · 315 / 300 pcs (+15)
  1. The number isn't cleared. The real count stays on screen, on purpose, so you can see which box put it over.
  2. Fix the field marked in red. Right there — no need to jump to another tab or pallet.
  3. Scan again. As soon as the total matches the target, the auto-jump picks up right where it left off.
